WEDDING INVITATION TEMPLATE V6 — SCRATCH DATE

The date and countdown are hidden under a scratch card.
The countdown does NOT calculate or run before the scratch card is revealed.
Once roughly half the scratch area is cleared:
- The scratch layer disappears.
- 12 DECEMBER 2026 is revealed.
- The countdown starts at that moment.

To change the wedding date, edit BOTH:
1. The visible date fields in the wedding object.
2. weddingDate in script.js.

Example:
const weddingDate = new Date("2026-12-12T09:00:00").getTime();
